About

The right people, in the same room.

Together WorX brings artists and big thinkers into the same room as people who rarely get the invitation.

Some nights it looks like a concert. Other nights it looks like a whole block painting one wall. Nobody comes to watch. The people in the room make the evening what it is, and they leave knowing each other.

That's been true since the first night, and it's what this new chapter is built on.

How it started

It started with a song in the park.

It started with a jog through Central Park. I heard voices so beautiful I honestly thought they were angels, and I followed them to Bethesda Fountain. It was the Boyd family, busking on a Sunday afternoon.

I was planning my very first Together WorX evening, and a friend had offered a beautiful space. All I needed was the music, and I knew it had to be them.

I asked. They weren't sure. I came back the next Sunday and asked again. Still nothing. The Sunday after that, they said yes.

That night a room full of strangers sat in candlelight and listened to a family the whole world would soon hear. The space was a gift. The music was a gift. Something opened in that room that I've been chasing ever since.

That's still how Together WorX works. I find people with something to give, and I keep asking until the right ones are in the same room. Over the years those rooms filled in New York and Los Angeles, and one gallery night became relief for the Bahamas after the hurricane.

This new chapter starts the same way. Someone out there has a gift to share. I'll keep asking.

Rebekah Hoyle, Founder
